# CS Girlies Annual Hackathon — Technology For Wellness

## Key info
- **Deadline:** Aug 16, 2026 @ 11:00pm GMT+1
- **Format:** Online, 48 hours
- **Theme:** Tools for wellness, health, and happiness
- **Prize pool:** $5,000 total

## Tracks
- **Happiness** (beginner) — lighthearted/story-based, e.g. compliment generator, kindness bingo, virtual pet stress reliever
- **Wellness** (intermediate) — e.g. hydration tracker, mood journal, stretch break timer, gratitude journal. If building a mood chatbot, must guard against negative-talk spirals.
- **Health** (advanced) — real health data tools, e.g. posture analyzer, voice-based mental health screener, medication tracker. Needs real security measures for health data.
- **Bonus — Most Viral:** most likes across social posts about the project ($250)
- **Bonus — Best Use of AI:** most creative use of AI *in code* (not art/docs) ($250)

## Submission requirements
1. **Demo video** (1–5 min, hosted on YouTube/Vimeo/Drive/etc.)
2. **Project description** — what it does, inspiration, tech used, AI usage + model + free/paid tier, track(s). Must be written from scratch, not AI-generated.
3. **Public code repo** (GitHub/Replit/Glitch) with a README/basic docs
4. **Team info** — all teammates added on Devpost with contributions noted
5. **Bonus track submissions** (if opted in) — social post links for Most Viral; detailed AI-usage report (prompts, models, paid/free) for Best Use of AI

## Prizes
- First Place Overall: $2,000
- Runner Up: $1,000
- Best In Wellness / Health / Happiness: $500 each
- Most Viral: $250
- Best Use of AI: $250

## Judging criteria
- Wellness Impact
- Creativity & Innovation
- Technical Craft & Execution
- Design & User Experience
- Community & Accessibility
